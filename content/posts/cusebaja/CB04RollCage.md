Writing on 2026-01-28, but everything mentioned here happened on 2026-01-23.

This past Friday, I along with the rest of the 'Cuse Baja chassis subteam started assembly of the frame for CB04, the brand new car for the team.

I have previously helped with measuring, cutting, and notching the tubes that make up the frame, but I was not able to help with the welding processes as I have not yet been taught how to weld.

I helped to move parts between the welding room and the main shop, prep tubes for welding, and set parts in the welding fixture.

There will most certainly be more of this in the future, as the roll cage is only one part of the frame and there is still quite a lot left to weld.
